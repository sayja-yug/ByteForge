Understanding Learning Progress

A quick guide to interpreting the metrics and reports on your dashboard.

📊

Scores & Mastery

Scores indicate percentage correctness on assessments.

  • 80-100% Mastery: Strong understanding.
  • 60-79% Proficient: Good, but needs review.
  • 0-59% Developing: Needs focused practice.
🔥

Day Streak

Shows consistency. A "streak" increases for every consecutive day your child completes at least one learning activity.

Consistency is key to long-term retention!
🛣️

The "Learning Path" is an AI-generated roadmap tailored to your child's diagnostic results. It adapts over time.
